Section 1
Opening the Tool
1
Extract the ZIP file
Right-click the downloaded ZIP and choose Extract All (Windows) or double-click (Mac). You'll see a folder containing auditpro-offline.html and this guide.
2
Open in Chrome or Edge
Double-click auditpro-offline.html. It opens directly in your browser — no server, no install. Chrome and Edge are recommended for best PDF export quality.
3
You're ready to audit
The Dashboard appears. Click Start First Audit → or Load Demo Audit to explore the tool with pre-filled sample data.
Tip: Bookmark the file in your browser for quick access. All your audit data is saved automatically in the browser's local storage — it persists between sessions even when offline.
Section 2
Running Your First Audit
1
Click "New Audit" in the sidebar
Enter the website URL (e.g. https://yoursite.com) and optionally a client name. These appear on the PDF report.
2
Work through each category
Use the category tabs at the top to navigate between the 5 audit areas. For each checkpoint, select one of four answers:
✓ Pass
~ Partial
✗ Fail
— N/A
3
Use the "Why it matters" tooltip
Each checkpoint has a ? icon. Hover or click it to see why the item matters and how to fix it. Great for sharing context with clients.
4
Click "Complete Audit →"
When you're done (or ready to save your progress), click Complete Audit →. Your score, grade, and results are saved to the Dashboard. You can always come back and edit.
You don't have to answer every checkpoint. Items you leave blank are excluded from the score. Partial audits generate a preliminary or directional reviewed score — letter grades are shown only when enough coverage has been completed (see Section 4 for details).

Section 3
White Label Setup & PDF Export
1
Go to White Label in the sidebar
Click the 🏷️ White Label PDF tab. Here you set your agency branding — it appears on every PDF you export.
2
Fill in your agency details
Enter your Agency Name, website, email, and optionally your logo (PNG or JPEG). Pick a Brand Color that matches your company identity.
3
Click "Save Settings"
Your branding is saved and applied to all future PDF exports. Settings persist between sessions.
4
Select the audit to export
In the Audit History list below the settings, click the audit you want to export. The active audit is highlighted.
5
Click "↓ PDF" to export
A branded PDF report is generated and downloaded instantly. The report includes a cover page, score summary, category breakdown, top issues, and a full checklist detail section.
PDF quality tip: For best PDF export quality, use Chrome or Edge and avoid closing the browser tab while the report is being generated.
Section 4
Scoring, Coverage & Data Backup
Coverage & Confidence

Coverage means how many applicable checkpoints have been reviewed. A low-coverage score reflects only the items completed so far — not the full website. Letter grades are only shown once you have reviewed enough checkpoints.

Coverage Score Label Confidence Grade
Below 30% Preliminary Reviewed Score Low Withheld
30 – 59% Directional Reviewed Score Medium-low Withheld
60 – 79% Indicative Score Medium Shown
80%+ Full Audit Score High Shown
How items are counted: Blank items are excluded entirely from the score — they do not count as passes or failures. Items marked N/A are excluded because they do not apply to this site. Only items marked Pass, Partial, or Fail are used to calculate the score.

Backup & Restore
  • 💾 All audit data is stored in your browser's local storage. It persists between sessions automatically.
  • ↓ Export Backup: Go to White Label → Data & Backup → Export Backup. Downloads a .json file you can save anywhere.
  • ↑ Import Backup: On a new device or after clearing data, use Import Backup to restore all your audits from the exported file.
  • ⚠️ Clearing browser data or switching browsers will erase local storage. Export a backup regularly.
Managing Multiple Audits
  • +Up to 20 audits stored simultaneously. Each has its own URL, client name, and results.
  • ✎Edit — re-open an audit and update any checkpoint.
  • ⧉New Blank Copy — create a blank audit with the same URL (useful for re-audits).
  • ↺Clear — reset all answers for an audit (hold 2 seconds to confirm).
  • ✕Delete — permanently remove an audit from history.
