# Salienzo naming test

Status: Ready to administer. No participants recruited or tested.

## Purpose

Assess pronunciation consistency, spelling from audio, and unaided next-day recall among English-speaking potential customers. This is a small exploratory test, not a representative market study or trademark clearance.

## Participants

Recruit 10 people who have not seen the name: 5 for Group A and 5 different people for Group B. Prefer prospective users in marketing, SEO, or SaaS. Record country and primary language. Do not explain the product or the intended meaning before testing. Do not include the name in recruitment messages or message subject lines.

## Recruitment message

Could you help with a two-minute brand-name test and one brief follow-up tomorrow? We are testing the name, not you. Please do not search for it online. Participation is optional.

## Group A: Reading

Show only this word in plain text:

Salienzo

Ask these questions in order. Do not suggest a pronunciation or correct answers.

1. How would you say this name aloud?
2. How easy was it to say, from 1 (very difficult) to 5 (very easy)?
3. What, if anything, does this name bring to mind?
4. Does it remind you of an existing brand? If so, which one?

Record pronunciation as heard; record audio only with permission. Remove the displayed name after this step.

## Group B: Hearing

Use the same spoken rendition for all five participants. Proposed rendition for this round: sa-lee-EN-zo, four syllables. This is a chosen brand pronunciation, not an established English pronunciation. Use audio or a call; never display the name or a filename containing it. Allow one replay and record whether requested.

1. How would you spell the name you just heard?
2. How easy was it to understand, from 1 (very difficult) to 5 (very easy)?
3. What, if anything, does this name bring to mind?
4. Does it remind you of an existing brand? If so, which one?

Do not reveal the correct spelling before the next-day follow-up.

## Follow-up for both groups

Send approximately 24 hours later in a message that does not expose the original name. Ask participants not to review previous messages.

Without looking back or searching, what was the brand name we tested yesterday? Please type what you remember. It is fine to say you do not remember.

After recall is recorded, ask:

The intended product helps businesses measure and improve their visibility in AI-generated answers. How well does the name fit that product, from 1 (not at all) to 5 (very well)? Why?

## Decision rules, fixed before testing

- Reading: at least 4/5 converge on a similar pronunciation. Note separately whether it matches the proposed spoken rendition; tolerate ordinary accent differences.
- Hearing: at least 4/5 spell Salienzo correctly, ignoring capitalization.
- Recall: at least 7/10 recall the name recognizably. Report exact spelling separately. Also report each group's recall separately because reading and hearing provide different cues.
- Report all mistakes and associations, not just the totals. Two or more independent mentions of the same competing brand warrant closer review, not automatic rejection.
- If follow-ups are missing, report missing counts and defer the recall decision. Do not silently change denominators or thresholds.
- These are internal screening thresholds, not validated scientific cutoffs. Results do not resolve domain availability or legal rights.
